# Monthly Revenue Descriptive Statistics

## Business Problem

A leadership team wants to evaluate the monthly revenue performance of two subscription products and understand both their overall revenue levels and month-to-month consistency during 2022.

Rather than reviewing a detailed monthly revenue table, the goal is to summarize the distribution of monthly revenue for each product using descriptive statistics.

## Relevant Data

The analysis uses two tables:

### `Subscriptions`

Contains subscription-level transaction information, including:

- `ProductID` — identifies the subscription product
- `OrderDate` — date of the subscription order
- `Revenue` — revenue generated by the subscription

### `Products`

Contains product information, including:

- `ProductID` — unique identifier for each product
- `ProductName` — name of the subscription product

## Objective

For each product, calculate the following statistics based on monthly revenue during 2022:

- Minimum monthly revenue
- Maximum monthly revenue
- Average monthly revenue
- Standard deviation of monthly revenue

These metrics provide a high-level view of both revenue performance and month-to-month variability.

## Approach

The solution first uses a Common Table Expression (CTE) to calculate total revenue by product and month.

The monthly revenue values are then aggregated by product to calculate:

- `MIN()` for the lowest monthly revenue
- `MAX()` for the highest monthly revenue
- `AVG()` for average monthly revenue
- `STDDEV()` for revenue variability

## SQL Concepts Used

- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- `JOIN`
- `DATE_TRUNC()`
- `SUM()`
- `MIN()`
- `MAX()`
- `AVG()`
- `STDDEV()`
- `GROUP BY`
